Frequently asked questions

Which coaching is best for UPSC?

Vajiram & Ravi is renowned for its comprehensive and structured approach, making it one of the top choices for UPSC coaching in India.

When should I start preparing for UPSC?

Graduates usually join the 1-year GS Prelims cum Mains Programme. College students can start earlier with the 2-year Fledgling programme, which spreads the syllabus across their degree.

What does the GS Prelims cum Mains Programme include?

Daily classes across the full General Studies syllabus, printed study material, Prelims and Mains tests, and mentoring, in the classroom or LIVE-Online.

Can I prepare in Hindi medium?

Yes. Vajiram & Ravi teaches in English and Hindi, with Hindi-medium mentorship and a Mains Test Series in Hindi.

Do you offer coaching for optional subjects?

Yes. Optional coaching runs for 20 to 30 weeks in subjects including Geography, History, Sociology, Psychology, Economics and Political Science, with an Optional Test Series alongside.

How do you prepare students for the UPSC interview?

The Interview Guidance Programme (IGP) prepares you for the Personality Test with mock interviews and DAF preparation, so you face the board with structure and confidence.

Can I join a test series without a full course?

Yes. The Prelims, Mains and Optional test series can be taken on their own, alongside your own preparation.
